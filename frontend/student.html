About KITCoEK

Kolhapur Institute of Technology's College of Engineering (Autonomous), Kolhapur was established in May 1983 by visionary engineers, industrialists, and educationists. KIT is recognized as the pioneer private autonomous engineering institute in Western Maharashtra, affiliated to Shivaji University, Kolhapur.

🏆 NAAC 'A+' Grade 🎖️ NBA Accredited 🏛️ Autonomous Institute 📜 AICTE Approved 🏷️ DTE Code: 6267

Campus & Smart Facilities

  • AI-Powered Attendance: CampusVision smart CCTV and biometric face scanning across lecture halls.
  • High-Performance Labs: Specialized NVIDIA GPU labs for CSE(AIML), Data Science, and Robotics.
  • Central Library: Over 60,000+ volumes, international digital journals, and e-learning resources.
  • Innovation Hub & Incubation: Active research cells and startup mentoring for budding engineers.
